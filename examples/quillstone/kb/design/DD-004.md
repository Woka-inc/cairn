# DD-004: Schema evolution

## Context

Readers and writers are deployed at different times, so they must tolerate one version of difference.

## Decision

Adding a field with a default and widening a numeric type are compatible; renames are two changes (add, then remove).

## Consequences

- The registry can answer compatibility questions offline.
- Renames take two releases.
