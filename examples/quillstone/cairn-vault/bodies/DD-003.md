# DD-003: Retry policy and idempotence

## Context

Automatic retries of non-idempotent writes produced duplicate rows in early users' pipelines.

## Decision

Only operations marked idempotent are retried automatically; sinks must use idempotent writes or checkpoints.

## Consequences

- Adapters declare idempotence explicitly.
- A per-pipeline retry budget prevents retry storms.
